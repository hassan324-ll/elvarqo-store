# Men collection landing page

Open http://localhost:3000/collections/men or click Men in the storefront menu.

Implemented from the approved Men mockup with the same responsive editorial styling as Women: seasonal hero, live category circles, olive strip, Styled for you, Trending now, Style that goes further campaign, What to wear, New arrivals, Good pieces on repeat campaign and the complete filtered catalogue. Existing header/footer and explicitly labelled newsletter preview remain.

## Dynamic content

Category names, order, visibility, links and images come from the active categories under Men. Styled for you selects the existing Coats & Jackets, Suits, T-Shirts & Polos, Knitwear, Footwear and Jeans categories. What to wear links Modern workwear to Shirts, Autumn essentials to Knitwear and Occasionwear to Suits. These sections use the corresponding category photos; inactive or removed categories do not produce invented tiles.

Trending now uses the existing public trending sort: confirmed paid GBP order quantities over the last 30 days, followed by newest first-publication date and product ID for ties. With no paid sales, recent published products appear automatically. New arrivals shows the four latest published Men products. Only products scoped to Men/direct assignments or active child categories are included. Existing filters, paging, wishlist, quick selection, prices and stock remain available. No sample products were added to the merchant database.

The visible page refreshes every 60 seconds and on tab return. Publish a product under a Men category to populate the sections; save alone leaves it private as a draft. Empty sections show empty states and failures show recovery controls. Campaign images/copy are fixed design content; category and product content is dynamic. No new banner CMS, live newsletter or payment integration was added.
